# Featured reel: Why does the poop emoji look like that?

`demo.mp4` is a 28.4-second English comedy explainer, remade with the repository's `create-reel` skill and portable Python renderer, and published here at the creator's request. `poster.jpg` is a frame from that video. English captions are part of the picture; there is no separate CC track.

- Visuals: seven original AI-generated fictional illustrations, created with OpenAI image generation for this production. No third-party stock artwork, identifiable people, or personal photographs are used.
- Voice: fresh synthesized English narration, generated through `edge-tts` using Microsoft's `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` voice. It is a synthetic voice, not a recording or clone of the creator or a friend. Caption timing uses actual provider word boundaries, with fragments of hyphenated words combined into the corresponding caption word.
- Soundtrack: narration only.
- Web delivery: H.264, 720 × 1280, 30 fps; AAC mono at 48 kHz. Narration is mastered to approximately −16 LUFS. The MP4 has its playback index at the front for streaming.

The English remake reuses the original text-free illustrations and renders new English captions over them. Its narration and timing were authored for English, rather than layered over the earlier Hebrew video. The starter in `examples/hello-reel/` remains the self-contained, offline renderer example.

Research sources retained from the original production:

- [The New Real, University of Minnesota Press](https://manifold.umn.edu/read/the-new-real/section/3443c736-d27d-4db1-9999-0bcdb2437a6c)
- [Emojipedia: Pile of Poo](https://emojipedia.org/pile-of-poo)
- [Vogue India: The secret origin of the poop emoji](https://www.vogue.in/content/the-secret-origin-of-the-poop-emoji)

The account of the Apple design workflow is attributed to an original designer; jokes about confidence, heritage, and a criminal past are comic interpretation.

Only the finished public example and its public credits are included. The working project, service state, and unrelated private files are outside this repository.
