# The Moon Is Ghosting Earth

Science comedy. The current average lunar recession rate of about 3.8 centimeters per year, measurement by lunar laser ranging, and the tidal transfer that slows Earth's rotation and enlarges the Moon's orbit were checked against the NASA/JPL sources below. The anthropomorphic characters, caliper, chair, and luminous transfer ribbon are illustrative jokes and visual metaphors; the scenes are not documentary photographs or scale diagrams. The story does not claim a sudden lunar departure.

Written and produced with the repository's **create-reel** skill and the portable ReelKit renderer. Five original illustrations were generated for this reel with the built-in image-generation tool. No third-party photographs, music, or sound effects are used.

English narration is synthetic: **en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural**, generated through edge-tts using Microsoft's online speech service. Captions are burned into the video and follow actual provider word-boundary timings. No separate CC track is embedded. Text uses the bundled Noto Sans font under the SIL Open Font License; its license and attribution are included in the repository.

Export: 720 × 1280, 30 fps, H.264 / AAC mono at 48 kHz. Narration is mastered near −16 LUFS with a −1.5 dBTP target.
