# Mars vs. the Unit Mix-Up — credits and factual notes

The 1999 mission loss and unit mismatch are historical facts. The approximately 4.45 factor refers to pound-force seconds versus newton-seconds in thruster impulse data. The review identified additional checking, engineering, and communication failures; this was not just one mistaken number. The laptop punchline, character acting, and illustrations are comedic inventions. Cost is intentionally omitted because published figures refer to different program scopes. Artwork is an interpretation, not archival NASA imagery.
