# The Shop That Sold Extra Minutes

An original fictional microstory about a magical clock shop, a father, a daughter, and a very bad paper airplane. The characters, shop, and events are invented; no historical or scientific claim is made.

Written and produced with the repository's **create-reel** skill and the portable ReelKit renderer. Five original illustrations were generated for this reel with the built-in image-generation tool. No third-party photographs, music, or sound effects are used.

English narration is synthetic: **en-US-EmmaMultilingualNeural**, generated through edge-tts using Microsoft's online speech service. Captions are burned into the video and follow actual provider word-boundary timings. No separate CC track is embedded. Text uses the bundled Noto Sans font under the SIL Open Font License; its license and attribution are included in the repository.

Export: 720 × 1280, 30 fps, H.264 / AAC mono at 48 kHz. Narration is mastered near −16 LUFS with a −1.5 dBTP target.
